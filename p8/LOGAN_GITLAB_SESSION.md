## Session record

Participant 3 opened the tablet prototype before a practice. They noticed and liked the **OFFLINE READY** badge because their stadium and rehearsal-hall Wi-Fi is poor. They selected **Open score** for “Movement I - Percussion.”

On the score viewer, they saw the previous/next controls, page indicator, Section B selector, and measures 17-24. They said they would tap measure 20. The prototype then displayed a saved state for measure 24; the participant treated this as a prototype mismatch rather than the intended selection.

The participant did not understand the corner star immediately: “I don't know what that is supposed to be.” After the saved message, they tried **Score Library** rather than proceeding to the cue flow. On the following screen, they selected **Save cue and review**. In the review screen, they understood that the measure had been saved and said it would be useful before practice to work through difficult measures. They liked the final resolved state: “I like this big green checkmark telling me that the measure is resolved. That makes me feel like I accomplished something.”

In post-task discussion, the participant said tapping could be confusing initially but, once learned, could be better than pencil marking. They found “add context after rehearsal” too specific because they anticipated using the app before rehearsal. When asked about several corrections, they expected to tap multiple measures.
