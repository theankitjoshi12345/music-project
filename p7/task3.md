# P#7 Task 3 - Critical Moment

Copy the fields below into the matching **Part 2: Critical Moment** table in the P#7 report template.

## The Moment

During rehearsal, a musician receives a correction for a difficult measure or transition and must be ready to play again almost immediately. The product is most valuable when the musician needs to preserve the correction now and revisit the exact passage later without missing the next entrance.

## Trigger

The director gives feedback about a specific measure or transition, then restarts the passage or moves on before the musician can write a complete note.

## Context

The musician is viewing the assigned score during an active rehearsal, where attention must alternate quickly between the conductor, the score, and the next entrance.

## User Goal

Capture enough context to return to the corrected passage during individual practice, apply the correction, and perform it confidently at the next run-through.

## Current Failure

Paper notes can take too long during a rapid rehearsal cycle. Generic score viewers can make annotation or navigation awkward and can clutter notation, while separate practice tools require the musician to switch tools or rely on memory.

## Your Solution

Keep score access, page or section navigation, quick measure markers, concise practice cues, and unresolved-passage review in one focused workflow. The musician can mark the relevant measure quickly, remain ready to play, and later return to a focused list of marked passages for practice.

## Evidence

“Often we don’t have enough time to pick up our pencils, write it down, and get ready to play again, because [the director]’s already conducting what he wants us to fix.” - Interview 7
