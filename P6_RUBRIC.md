# P#6 Grader Notes — Applying the Rubric

Use this guide to review the P#6 report before submission. It summarizes the
grader notes supplied for the assignment; it does not authorize changes to
GitLab, GitHub, Moodle, or the final PDF.

## Important requirement mismatch

The assignment instructions specify **1–2 scenarios per persona**, while the
grader-notes table asks whether **2–3 scenarios** are present. Do not silently
invent or pad scenarios to resolve this difference. Use 1–2 well-supported
scenarios per persona unless the instructor provides a clarification; if time
and evidence allow, a third scenario may be added only when it is distinct,
supported by interview data, and improves the report.

| Component | Evidence-Based | Logical Flow | Critical Thinking | Clarity and Completeness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Segments | Grounded in interview patterns; quotes cited | Users partitioned meaningfully | Behavioral, not demographic | 2–3 segments present and clearly described |

| GitLab | — | — | Issues used meaningfully | Milestone, issues, and assignments are present |

## Grader workflow

1. Check structure: all sections are present and individual contributions are
   labeled with member codes.
2. Read segments: verify that they come from interview patterns and are
   behavioral.
3. Read personas: verify that each maps to a segment and uses a real quote.
4. Read scenarios: verify that they flow from persona goals and pains and have
   specific triggers.
5. Read user stories: trace each back to a scenario and check the required
   format.
6. Read features: verify logical grouping from stories and the presence of
   excluded features.
7. Read assumptions: verify that they are meaningful and show thoughtful risk
   assessment.
8. Check GitLab: verify that the milestone exists and that issues are assigned
   and closed when the related work is complete.

## Scoring guidance

- Score the four criteria holistically, not one component at a time.
- A weakness in one component can be offset by strength in another.
- Evaluate the overall quality of synthesis, not only checklist completion.
- Excluded features and meaningful assumptions are evidence of productive
  scope discipline and critical thinking.

## Performance levels

### 1. Evidence-Based — 25%

| Level | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | Segments clearly derive from interview patterns; personas include authentic quotes; scenarios reflect real situations. |
| Good | Most artifacts connect to interview data; some quotes or evidence are cited. |
| Adequate | General connection to interviews, but specific evidence is limited; some personas feel invented. |
| Weak | Artifacts appear made up, with little connection to interview findings. |

### 2. Logical Flow — 25%

| Level | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | Clear traceability: segments → personas → scenarios → stories → features. |
| Good | Generally coherent, with minor gaps in traceability. |
| Adequate | Some connections exist, but it is difficult to trace features back to user needs. |
| Weak | Artifacts are disconnected; features do not clearly map to stories or personas. |

### 3. Critical Thinking — 25%

| Level | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | Segments are behavioral; exclusions show discipline; assumptions are meaningful; risk assessment shows judgment. |
| Good | Good decisions overall; some assumptions could be more specific. |
| Adequate | Basic critical thinking; assumptions are somewhat generic; few exclusions. |
| Weak | Little critical evaluation; no exclusions; assumptions are trivial or missing. |

### 4. Clarity and Completeness — 25%

| Level | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | All sections are present, organized, and easy to follow; contributions are labeled; GitLab milestone and issues are in place. |
| Good | All sections are present, with minor organizational issues; GitLab is partially used. |
| Adequate | Some sections are incomplete or unclear; GitLab use is minimal. |
| Weak | Sections are missing or disorganized; GitLab evidence is absent. |

## Final review checklist

- [ ] Evidence is real, traceable, and free of invented quotes or observations.
- [ ] The segment-to-feature chain can be followed without guesswork.
- [ ] Every individual contribution has a member code.
- [ ] User stories use the required `As a …, I want to …, so that …` form.
- [ ] The feature list includes justified excluded features.
- [ ] Exactly three risky, testable assumptions are present.
- [ ] The document is complete and consistently formatted before PDF export.
- [ ] GitLab tracking reflects the team’s actual completion status.
